### Concept - 3: Finding the Base

**Result no. given, % is given, Base is asked.**

> **Concept Note:**
> If the base is not given, take that as 100% or any number as per your convenience. Instead of using long formulas, simply equate the given percentage to the actual given number.
>
> **The Master Rule:**
>
> * **What % = Given actual number**
> * **100% = ?**
> * **Required % = ?**

### Profit & Loss Questions

**1. A man sold an article for ₹240 at a profit of 20%. Find his cost price.**  
(a) ₹192 &nbsp; &nbsp; (b) ₹288 &nbsp; &nbsp; (c) ₹200 &nbsp; &nbsp; (d) ₹224

**Ans:-**

* **Base (Cost Price):** 100%
* **Profit:** 20%
* **Selling Price (SP):** 100% + 20% = 120%

Applying the rule (What % = Given actual number):  
120 % = 240  
100 % $= \frac{240}{120} \times 100 = 200$

**Correct Option: (c)**

### **Summary of Concepts (Profit & Loss):**

* **Concept-1 (Finding Profit):**  
If CP = 300 (Base) and P = 20%,  
then Profit = $300 \times \frac{20}{100} = 60$. SP = 360.

* **Concept-2 (Finding %):**  
If CP = 300 and Profit = 60,  
then Profit % = $\frac{60}{300} \times 100 = 20$%.

* **Concept-3 (Finding Base):**  
If SP = 360 and P = 20%, CP = 100% and SP = 120%.  
120% $= 360 \implies$ 100% $= \frac{360}{120} \times 100 = 300$.

### Comparison: More than / Less than

Understanding how the base shifts depending on what is being compared:

* **What percent is 30 more than 25?**  
  Difference = 5. Base = 25.  
  $\frac{5}{25} \times 100 = 20$%

* **What percent is 25 less than 30?**  
  Difference = 5. Base = 30.  
  $\frac{5}{30} \times 100 = 16.66$%

* **A is 20% more than B. B is what % less than A?**  
  Let B = 100. A = 120.  
  Difference = 20. Base = A (120).  
  $\frac{20}{120} \times 100 = 16.66$%

* **A & B are 20% & 50% more than C respectively. A is what % less than B?**  
  Let C = 100. A = 120. B = 150.  
  Difference = 30. Base = B (150).  
  $\frac{30}{150} \times 100 = 20$%

**2. A man sold an article for ₹240 at a profit of 11.11%. Find his cost price.**  
(a) ₹192 &nbsp; &nbsp; (b) ₹288 &nbsp; &nbsp; (c) ₹200 &nbsp; &nbsp; (d) ₹216

**Ans:-**

*When the percentage is a complex decimal like 11.11%, always use the fraction method instead of taking the base as 100%.*

* 11.11% in fraction = $\frac{1}{9}$
* **Base (Cost Price):** 9 parts
* **Selling Price (SP):** $9 + 1 = 10$ parts

Equating the parts to actual numbers:  
$10 \text{ parts} = 240$  
$9 \text{ parts} = \frac{240}{10} \times 9 = 216$

**Correct Option: (d)**

### Simple Interest Questions

**3. A certain sum amounts to ₹6500 in 5 years at 6% p.a. simple interest. Find the sum.**  
(a) ₹8450 &nbsp; &nbsp; (b) ₹8455 &nbsp; &nbsp; (c) ₹5000 &nbsp; &nbsp; (d) ₹6000

**Ans:-**

> **Formula Method (Time-Consuming - Avoid):**  
> $\text{Amount} = \text{Principal} + \text{Simple Interest}$  
> $P + \frac{P \times R \times T}{100} = 6500$  
> $P + \frac{P \times 6 \times 5}{100} = 6500 \implies \frac{130P}{100} = 6500 \implies P = 5000$

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/PSCodersHub/PLACEMENT/raw/main/Assets/49.jpg" alt="Simple Interest Formula Approach" width="500">
</div>

> **Shortcut Method (Recommended):**  
> In SI & CI, always treat the Principal as 100%.  
> Total Simple Interest % = $(R \times T)$%  
> Total Amount % = $(100 + R \times T)$%

* $SI = (6 \times 5) = 30$%
* $\text{AMOUNT}$ = 100 % + 30 % = 130 %

Applying the rule (What % = Given actual number):  
130 % = 6500  
100 % = 5000

**Correct Option: (c)**

### Finding the Base: Examination Questions

**4. In an examination, a student has to get 35% to pass. He got 30% and fails by 20 marks. Find the maximum mark.**  
(a) 200 &nbsp; &nbsp; (b) 300 &nbsp; &nbsp; (c) 400 &nbsp; &nbsp; (d) 500

**Ans:-**

* Required Pass % = 35%
* Student's Score % = 30%
* Percentage Gap = 35% - 30% = 5%

This 5% gap is exactly equal to the 20 marks he failed by.  
5% = 20  
100% = 400

**Correct Option: (c)**

**5. A student scoring 25% fails by 30 marks. Another student scoring 40% gets 60 marks above the pass mark. Find the Maximum Marks.**

**Ans:-**

Find the total gap in their percentages and equate it to the total gap in their actual marks.

* **Student A:** 25% (30 marks below pass)
* **Student B:** 40% (60 marks above pass)
* Difference in % = 40% - 25% = 15%
* Difference in marks = 60 + 30 = 90

15% = 90  
Hence, 100% = 600

**Maximum Marks = 600**

### Finding the Base: Election Questions

**6. At an election contested by two candidates a candidate got 70% of the total votes and won the election by a majority of 16,000 votes. Find the total number of votes.**  
(a) 40,000 &nbsp; &nbsp; (b) 30,000 &nbsp; &nbsp; (c) 50,000 &nbsp; &nbsp; (d) 35,000

**Ans:-**

* Winning Candidate (W) = 70%
* Losing Candidate (L) = 30%
* Majority (Difference) = 70% - 30% = 40%

This majority percentage equals the actual majority vote count:  
40% = 16000  
100% = 40000

**Correct Option: (a)**

### Data Interpretation: Pie Charts

**7. What is the central angle of the sector corresponding to the expenditure incurred on Royalty?**

**Ans:-**
In a pie chart, the full circle is 360 degrees.

* 100% $= 360^\circ$
* 1% $= 3.6^\circ$

Royalty is 15%:  
Central Angle = $15 \times 3.6 = 54^\circ$

**8. If for a certain quantity of books, the publisher has to pay Rs. 30,600 as printing cost, then what will be amount of royalty to be paid for these books?**

**Ans:-**
We use the direct ratio method from Concept-3.

* Printing Cost % = 20%
* Royalty % = 15%

20% $= 30600$  
15% $= \frac{30600}{20} \times 15 = 22950$

Amount of royalty = **Rs. 22950**
