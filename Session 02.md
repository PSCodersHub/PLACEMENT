**Q. A man sold an article for ₹600 at profit of 20%. Find his cost price.**

**Ans:-**

The Cost Price (CP) of an article is always considered as **100%**.  
Since the man made a profit of **20%**, the Selling Price (SP) becomes:  

SP = 100% + 20% = **120% of CP**  

We are told that the Selling Price is ₹600.  
Therefore,  
120% of CP = ₹600  

To find the Cost Price, we calculate:  
CP = (600 ÷ 120) × 100  
CP = ₹500 

The **Cost Price (CP)** of the article is **₹500**.

**2. A man buys an article for Rs 120. At How much Profit will he get by selling at 20% Profit?**

**Ans:-**
The **Cost Price (CP)** of the article is ₹120.  
The profit percentage is given as **20%**.  

Profit is calculated using the formula:  
Profit = (Cost Price × Profit %) ÷ 100 

Substituting the values:  
Profit = $120 \times \frac{20}{100}$  
Profit = 24  

The man will earn a **profit of ₹24**.

### Simple Interest

**3. Find the simple interest on Rs 5000 in 3 years at 10% p.a.**

**Ans:-**

The **Principal (P)** is **₹5000**.  
The **Rate of Interest (R)** is **10% per annum**.  
The **Time (T)** is **3 years**.

First, we find the Simple Interest for 1 year (**10% of ₹5000**):

Simple Interest for 1 year  
= $5000 \times \frac{10}{100}$  
= ₹500

Now, we find the Simple Interest for 3 years:

Simple Interest for 3 years  
= $500 \times 3$  
= ₹1500

The Simple Interest is **₹1500**.

### Compound Interest

**5. Find the compound interest on Rs 5000 in 3 years at 10% p.a?**

**Ans:-**
* $5000 \times \frac{10}{100} = 500 + 5000 = 5500$  [1st year]
* $5500 \times \frac{10}{100} = 550 + 5500 = 6050$  [2nd year]
* $6050 \times \frac{10}{100} = 605 + 6050 = 6655$  [3rd year]

Compound Interest (CI) = 6655 - 5000 = **1655**

**6. Find the compound interest on Rs 6000 in $1\frac{1}{2}$ years at 10% p.a. if interest is compounded half-yearly?**

**Ans:-**

- Principal (P) = ₹6000  
- Rate of Interest (R) = 10% per annum  
- Since interest is compounded half‑yearly, the rate for each half‑year = 10% ÷ 2 = **5%**  
- Time = $1\frac{1}{2}$ years = 18 months = 3 half‑years

* $6000 \times \frac{5}{100} = 300 + 6000 = 6300$ [6 month]
* $6300 \times \frac{5}{100} = 315 + 6300 = 6615$ [6 month]
* $6615 \times \frac{5}{100} = 330.75 + 6615 = 6945.75$ [6 month]

Compound Interest (CI) = 6945.75 - 6000 = **945.75**

### Comparative Examples

**Concept Explanation:** Both Profit & Loss and Simple Interest use a "Base" value (Cost Price or Principal). To find the final total (Selling Price or Amount), you can either calculate the increase and add it to the base, OR use a direct percentage multiplier for a faster calculation.

#### 1. Profit and Loss [Example]
* **Base (Cost Price, CP):** 120
* **Rate of Profit:** 20%

**Method A: Traditional Calculation**
First, find the profit amount, then add it to the base.
* Profit = Cost Price $\times \frac{\text{Rate}}{100}$ 
* Profit = $120 \times \frac{20}{100}$
* Profit = $\frac{12 \times 20}{10}$ = $\frac{240}{10}$ = **24**
* Selling Price (SP) = Cost Price + Profit
* SP = 120 + 24 = **144**

**Method B: Direct Multiplier (As shown in diagram)**
Since there is a 20% profit, the Selling Price is exactly **120% of the Cost Price**. 

* **Formula Structure:** $\text{SP} = \text{Base Value (CP)} \times \frac{100\% + \text{Profit}\%}{100\%}$
* **Breaking down the numbers:**
  * **$120$ (First number):** The actual Cost Price (the Base).
  * **$120$ (Numerator):** This is $(100 + 20)$. The original cost is always considered 100%, and you are adding the 20% profit to it.
  * **$100$ (Denominator):** This represents the base percentage, used to convert the percentage back into a decimal/fraction.
* **Calculation:** 
  SP = $120 \times \frac{120}{100} = 144$

---

#### 2. Simple Interest [Example]
* **Base (Principal):** 5000
* **Rate:** 10% per annum

**Method A: Traditional Calculation**
First, find the simple interest, then add it to the base.
* Simple Interest in 1 year = $5000 \times \frac{10}{100} = \textbf{500}$
* Simple Interest in 3 years = $500 \times 3 = \textbf{1500}$
* Final Amount = Principal + SI
* Final Amount = 5000 + 1500 = **6500**

**Method B: Direct Multiplier (As shown in diagram)**
For 3 years at 10% per year, the total accumulated interest is 30%. Therefore, the final Amount is exactly **130% of the initial Principal**. 

* **Formula Structure:** $\text{Amount} = \text{Base Value (Principal)} \times \frac{100\% + \text{Total Interest}\%}{100\%}$
* **Breaking down the numbers:**
  * **$5000$ (First number):** The actual Principal amount (the Base).
  * **$130$ (Numerator):** This is $(100 + 30)$. The original principal is always 100%, and you are adding the total 30% interest accumulated over 3 years.
  * **$100$ (Denominator):** This represents the base percentage, used to convert the percentage back into a fraction.
* **Calculation:** 
  Amount in 3 yrs = $5000 \times \frac{130}{100} = 6500$
