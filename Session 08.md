### Concept - 4: Line Method (Inverse Proportion)

**Used for questions where A × B = Constant (e.g., Area, Expenditure).**

> **Concept Note:**
> Instead of calculating actual values, use the fraction-based "Line Method".
> * If there is an **INCREASE** (More/Raise/Exceed/Greater) by a fraction $\frac{x}{y}$,
> * The corresponding **DECREASE** (Less/Reduce/Fall/Shorter) to maintain the constant is $\frac{x}{x+y}$.
>
> Conversely:
> * If there is a **DECREASE** by $\frac{x}{y}$,
> * The corresponding **INCREASE** is $\frac{x}{y-x}$.

### Application-1 (Change of Base)

**1. The length and breadth of a rectangle are 40 cms and 20 cms respectively, If the length increases by 25%, by what % must the breadth be decreased so as to maintain the same area?**  
(a) 16 &nbsp; &nbsp; (b) 4 &nbsp; &nbsp; (c) 80 &nbsp; &nbsp; (d) 20

**Ans:-**

> **Long Method (Time-Consuming - Avoid):**  
> Area = $40 \times 20 = 800 \text{ cm}^2$  
> Increase in length = $40 \times \frac{25}{100} = 10$  
> New length = $40 + 10 = 50$ cms  
> New breadth = $800 / 50 = 16$ cms  
> Decrease = $20 - 16 = 4$ cms $\implies \frac{4}{20} \times 100 = 20$%

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/PSCodersHub/PLACEMENT/raw/main/Assets/62.jpg" alt="Long Method Application" width="500">
</div>

> **Shortcut Method (Line Method):**  
> Ignore actual lengths.
> * Increase in length = 25% = $\frac{1}{4}$ ($\frac{x}{y}$)
> * Decrease in breadth = $\frac{x}{x+y} = \frac{1}{1+4} = \frac{1}{5}$
> * $\frac{1}{5} \times 100 = 20$%

**Correct Option: (d)**

**3. Ram's salary is 10% less than Sam's salary. Sam's salary is what % more than Ram's salary?**

**Ans:-**
* Decrease = 10% = $\frac{1}{10}$ ($\frac{x}{y}$)
* Increase = $\frac{x}{y-x} = \frac{1}{10-1} = \frac{1}{9}$
* $\frac{1}{9} = 11\frac{1}{9}$% = 11.11%

### Concept - 5: Successive Change

**8. The length and breadth of a rectangle are 40 cms and 20 cms respectively, If the length increases by 25% and breadth increases by 20%, by what % will the area increase?**  
(a) 45% &nbsp; &nbsp; (b) 40% &nbsp; &nbsp; (c) 50% &nbsp; &nbsp; (d) 60%

**Ans:-**

> **Long Method (Time-Consuming - Avoid):**  
> Area = $40 \times 20 = 800 \text{ cm}^2$  
> Increase in length = $40 \times \frac{25}{100} = 10$  
> Increase in breadth = $20 \times \frac{20}{100} = 4$  
> New length = $40 + 10 = 50$ cms  
> New breadth = $20 + 4 = 24$ cms  
> New area = $50 \times 24 = 1200 \text{ cm}^2$  
> Increase in Area = $1200 - 800 = 400 \text{ cm}^2$  
> % increase = $\frac{400}{800} \times 100 = 50$%

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/PSCodersHub/PLACEMENT/raw/main/Assets/72.jpg" alt="Successive Change Example" width="500">
</div>

> **Shortcut Method:**  
> Successive Increase Formula: $A + B + \frac{A \times B}{100}$
> * Step 1 (Plug in the percentages): $25 + 20 + \frac{25 \times 20}{100}$
> * Step 2 (Solve the multiplication): $25 + 20 + \frac{500}{100}$
> * Step 3 (Simplify the fraction): $25 + 20 + 5$
> * Step 4 (Add them up): $45 + 5 = 50$%

**Correct Option: (c)**
