### FORMULAS: AB Method (Generalized Successive % Change)

**Used for any two quantities P and Q whose product is R (e.g., Area, Revenue, Expenditure) — a generalized version of the Line Method.**

### Application - 1 (Two Quantities – Product Rule: P × Q = R)

> **Case 1 — Both quantities increase:**  
> If **P increases by A%** and **Q increases by B%**, then:  
> $$\text{Net \% increase in Product} = A + B + \frac{A \times B}{100}$$

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/PSCodersHub/PLACEMENT/raw/main/Assets/85.jpg" alt="AB Method Application-1 Formula" width="500">
</div>

> **Case 2 — Both quantities decrease:**  
> If **P decreases by A%** and **Q decreases by B%**, then:  
> $$\text{Net \% change in Product} = -A - B + \frac{A \times B}{100}$$

> **Case 3 — One increases, one decreases:**  
> - **Sub‑case (a):**  
> If **P increases by A%** and **Q decreases by B%**, then:  
> $$\text{Net \% change in Product} = A - B - \frac{A \times B}{100}$$
> *(A positive result = net increase, a negative result = net decrease)*
>
> - **Sub‑case (b):**  
> If **P decreases by A%** and **Q increases by B%**, then:  
> $$\text{Net \% change in Product} = B - A - \frac{A \times B}{100}$$
> *(A positive result = net increase, a negative result = net decrease)*

**1. The length and breadth of a rectangle are 40 cms and 20 cms respectively, If the length increases by 25% and breadth increases by 20%, by what % will the area increase?**  
(a) 45% &nbsp; &nbsp; (b) 40% &nbsp; &nbsp; (c) 50% &nbsp; &nbsp; (d) 60%

**Ans:-**

> **Long Method (Time-Consuming - Avoid):**  
> Area = $40 \times 20 = 800 \text{ cm}^2$  
> Increase in length = $40 \times \frac{25}{100} = 10$  
> Increase in breadth = $20 \times \frac{20}{100} = 4$  
> New length = $40 + 10 = 50$ cms  
> New breadth = $20 + 4 = 24$ cms  
> New area = $50 \times 24 = 1200 \text{ cm}^2$  
> Increase in Area = $1200 - 800 = 400 \text{ cm}^2$  
> % increase = $\frac{400}{800} \times 100 = 50$%

<div align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/PSCodersHub/PLACEMENT/raw/main/Assets/72.jpg" alt="Successive Change Example" width="500">
</div>

> **Shortcut Method:**  
> Successive Increase Formula: $A + B + \frac{A \times B}{100}$
> * Step 1 (Plug in the percentages): $25 + 20 + \frac{25 \times 20}{100}$
> * Step 2 (Solve the multiplication): $25 + 20 + \frac{500}{100}$
> * Step 3 (Simplify the fraction): $25 + 20 + 5$
> * Step 4 (Add them up): $45 + 5 = 50$%

**Correct Option: (c)**

</div>

**2. The side of a square increases by 10%. By what % will the area increase?**

**Ans:-**

- Area = side × side, so both "quantities" increase by the same 10% (A = B = 10)
- Net % increase $= 10 + 10 + \dfrac{10 \times 10}{100} = 21$%

**3. The length of a rectangle increases by 20%, but the breadth decreases by 10%, by what % will the area change?**

**Ans:-**

- Increase = 20% (A), Decrease = 10% (B)
- Net % change $= 20 - 10 - \dfrac{20 \times 10}{100} = 8$% (8% increase)

---

**4. The price of a motor bike increases by 40%, while the sales of it decreases by 30%. What is the percentage change in the total revenue?**

**Ans:-**

- Increase = 40% (A), Decrease = 30% (B)
- Net % change $= 40 - 30 - \dfrac{40 \times 30}{100} = -2$% (2% decrease)

---

**5. The value of an article is decreased by 10% and again decreased by 10%. What is the net effect on its price?**

**Ans:-**

- Decrease = 10% (A), Decrease = 10% (B)
- Net % change $= -10 - 10 + \dfrac{10 \times 10}{100} = -19$% (19% decrease)

---

> - **Successive increase of 10% thrice**

- Step 1: $10 + 10 + \dfrac{10 \times 10}{100} = 21$%
- Step 2: $21 + 10 + \dfrac{21 \times 10}{100} = 33.1$%

### Using AB Method Twice, Thrice… (Three or More Quantities)

**6. The length, breadth, and height of a cuboid increase by 20%, 10%, and 10% respectively. By what % will the volume increase?**

**Ans:-**
- Step 1 (combine first two): $20 + 10 + \dfrac{20 \times 10}{100} = 32$
- Step 2 (combine with third): $32 + 10 + \dfrac{32 \times 10}{100} = 45.2$%

### Application - 3 (Successive Discount by AB Method)

**8. Two successive discounts of 20% and 25% are equivalent to a single discount of what %?**

**Ans:-**

- Discounts are decreases: A = 20, B = 25
- Net % change $= -20 - 25 + \dfrac{20 \times 25}{100} = -40$%
- Equivalent to a single **40% discount**

### Application - 4 (Population Problems)

**9. The population of a town is 50000. If it increases by 10% every year, what will be the population after 3 years?**

**Ans:-**

> **Method - 2 (using AB Method net %):**
> - 3 successive 10% increases = 33.1% net increase (from above)
> - Increase in population $= 50000 \times \dfrac{33.1}{100} = 16550$
> - Population after 3 years $= 50000 + 16550 = 66550$
>
> **OR directly:**
> - Population after 3 years $= 50000 \times \dfrac{133.1}{100} = 66550$

**10. The population of a town is 72600. If it increases by 10% every year, what was the population 2 years ago?**  
(a) 87646 &nbsp; &nbsp; (b) 58606 &nbsp; &nbsp; (c) 57354 &nbsp; &nbsp; (d) 60000

**Ans:-**

> **Method - 3 (Reverse using AB Method %):**
> - 2 years of 10% growth = 21% net increase (from AB Method)
> - So current population = 121% of the population 2 years ago
> - $121\% = 72600 \implies 100\% = 72600 \times \dfrac{100}{121} = 60000$

**Correct Option: (d)**

### Application - 5 (Compound Interest using AB Method)

**11. Find the compound interest on ₹6000 in 3 years at 10% p.a.**

**Ans:-**

- Net % increase over 3 years at 10% (from AB Method) = 33.1%
- Compound Interest $= 6000 \times \dfrac{33.1}{100} = ₹1986$

**12. Find the compound interest on ₹4000 in 2 years at 10% p.a.**

**Ans:-**
- Net % increase over 2 years at 10% (from AB Method) = 21%
- Compound Interest $= 4000 \times \dfrac{21}{100} = ₹840$
